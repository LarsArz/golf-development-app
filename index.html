Chipping • Pressure Game

Low, Medium, High

One target. Three trajectories. Three up-and-downs.

Setup

Pick a hole roughly 30 feet away. Use the same target for the entire game.

How To Play

Hit one chip using each of the three required trajectories:

↗
LOW
⤴
MEDIUM
⬆
HIGH

Finish The Shot

After each chip, play the ball out. The chip only counts when you get the ball up-and-down.

The Goal
Successfully complete the Low, Medium and High up-and-downs in the same run.
Miss any of the three up-and-downs? Start the game over from Low.
📵 Know the game? Put the phone away and go play.
